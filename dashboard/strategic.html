AI Scoring Methodology

1. Kinematic Projection: Fits a 1st or 2nd-degree polynomial (np.polyfit) to the last 5 years of satellite telemetry. Upgrades to a quadratic (2nd-degree) fit if structural acceleration is detected to prevent underestimating non-linear collapse.

2. Conservative Screening Threshold: Solves for the exact month this trend line intersects the hazard's carrying capacity (e.g., 150% GLOF expansion, or 10% total land loss).

3. Forest Penalty: Normalizes hazards into a 0-1 composite score. Habitations with >10% recent forest loss receive an automatic 15% risk penalty.
