This is a very common issue when working with FastAPI and file uploads! Here is exactly what is happening in those logs:

1. **`POST /damage` -> `307 Temporary Redirect`**: Your frontend is making a `POST` request to `/damage` (without a trailing slash). However, in your `backend/api/damage.py` file, the route is defined as `@router.post("/")` with a prefix of `/damage`, which means FastAPI strictly expects the URL to be `/damage/` (with the trailing slash). Because it's missing the slash, FastAPI sends a `307 Redirect` to tell the frontend to try again at `/damage/`.
2. **`POST /damage/` -> `422 Unprocessable Entity`**: Your frontend's HTTP client (like `fetch` or `axios`) automatically follows the redirect. However, when browsers automatically follow a `307 Redirect` on a `POST` request, they often **drop the `multipart/form-data` payload (your files)**. So, the second request hits `/damage/`, but the `pre_image` and `post_image` files are missing! FastAPI then throws a `422 Unprocessable Entity` error because it requires those files.

### How to fix it

You have two options, but **Option 1 is the most recommended** best practice:

**Option 1: Fix the Frontend URL (Recommended)**
